Chess vocabulary (English practice)
to develop a piece
to bring a knight or bishop off its starting square, into the game.
to castle
the special king + rook move that makes your king safe.
to hang a piece
to leave a piece undefended, so the opponent can win it for free.
to capture / to take
to remove an opponent's piece: "I'll take the knight."
to defend / to attack
a defended piece can be recaptured; an attacked piece is in danger.
the centre
the four middle squares (d4, e4, d5, e5) — controlling them is a key goal.
a blunder
a very bad move that loses material or the game.
check / checkmate
the king is attacked / the king is trapped and the game ends.
a pin / a fork
tactics: a pinned piece can't move; a fork attacks two things at once.
tempo
a unit of time; "to lose a tempo" means to waste a move.
